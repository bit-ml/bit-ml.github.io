direction: deepfake
---

We focus on advancing deepfake detection across video and audio modalities.
Our research is guided by three goals:
(i) Generalization: develop methods that transfer across diverse datasets and forgery techniques.
(ii) Transparency: understand how detection models make decisions and ensure datasets are reliable (free of spurious shortcuts).
(iii) Deployability: build systems that adapt and remain robust on unconstrained "in-the-wild" content.
